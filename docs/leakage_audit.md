## Design rules enforced in code

- Decision time for bar t is its close (ts + 1 min); the trade executes at the VWAP of bar t+1 (`backtest.py`), never at the price that produced the signal.
- All rolling windows are trailing (`rolling_*` in Polars end at the current row); no `shift(-k)` appears in `features.py` (negative shifts exist only in `targets.py`).
- No backward fill anywhere; forward fill only for the last traded price.
- Regime cut-offs and the large-trade size threshold are estimated on the initial training window (2023-03-01 .. 2024-02-29) only.
- Holdout rows are only reachable through `validation.unlock_holdout`, which refuses without `docs/final_methodology.md` and logs every access with that file's SHA-256 (`reports/holdout_access_log.jsonl`).
